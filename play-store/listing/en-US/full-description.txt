ToastLift is a focused workout generator that removes planning friction without hiding the signal behind your training.

Generate a session
Choose a duration, focus, and equipment. ToastLift builds a practical workout you can adjust before you begin.

Train what matters now
Training freshness uses your recent completed work to show what deserves attention next. Weekly muscle targets help you spot under-served areas without turning every workout into a spreadsheet.

Explore with clarity
Browse a structured exercise library with filters, favorites, experience levels, movement types, and training history.

Keep momentum visible
Log completed sets, review workout history, and use the lightweight token system as a record of showing up.

Private by default
The first release has no accounts, ads, analytics, AI features, cloud sync, or app network access. Workout data stays on your device. Export your data when you want a copy, or delete it from the app.

ToastLift is deliberately small: focused training, useful context, and fewer decisions between you and your next session.
