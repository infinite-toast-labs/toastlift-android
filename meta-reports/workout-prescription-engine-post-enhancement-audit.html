Remaining Constraints

The generator is still a session generator. It now handles the audited high/medium issues, but it still does not replace a periodized program: no mesocycle targets, planned deloads, or future-session reservation unless the app uses ProgramSessionContext.

Work-unit-only conditioning history remains protected from strength prescriptions. A future improvement would add a separate conditioning stimulus channel rather than mixing duration or distance work into rep-based load logic.
